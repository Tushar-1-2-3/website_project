About InAmigos

A licensed NGO with a people-first mission.

Founded on 23rd September 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a government-licensed NGO headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, India. Registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 with CIN U85300CT2020NPL010641 and PAN AAFCI8183C, the foundation works to unite minds for change across hunger relief, education, environment, women empowerment, animal welfare, and community development.

Leadership: Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO; Raman Swarnakar, Chief Operating Officer; Khush Gupta, Deputy Head and Core Member; Iniya Radhakrishnan, Head of the Internship Department.

Social Impact

The Numbers Behind Our Mission

Food Drives include daily meals, nutritional kits, community kitchens, and dry ration distribution for families who need immediate support.

Social Work supports sponsored education, health camps, vocational training, and dignified access to opportunity.

Charity Drives bring winter clothes, medical support, and school supplies to communities across need zones.

Cleanliness Drives promote street cleaning, dustbin installation, and plastic-free campaigns for healthier neighborhoods.
